Planned automatic matching

A photo will be analyzed into individual book regions. For each region, the importer will propose an existing book, an edition or a new entry. High-confidence matches will update safe fields; uncertain ones will appear in a report. Undo will preserve the earlier values.

Imports & Undo

Special-edition covers

Reversible updates
