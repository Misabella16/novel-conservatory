For visitors

How to use the site.

  1. Browse. Start with Discover or the catalogue. Search or filter; choose List View or Cover View. In filters, select included story tags and use Exclude story tags to hide unwanted tropes.
  2. Choose. Open a novel card for the synopsis, tags, translator information and available outside links.
  3. Save. Tap the heart or open Collections to keep interesting stories for later.
  4. Participate. Open a novel's community section to share your thoughts or permitted contributions.
  5. Suggest a missing title. Submit its details for editorial review.

Help the library grow

Three ways to contribute.

✎ Suggest a novel

Tell us about a missing story. Add its title, author, original or authorized synopsis, and a source link. New catalogue entries are reviewed before publication.

Suggest a novel ↗

💬 Add to a novel

Open a novel → Reader reviews & suggestions → Add story tags directly. Enter comma-separated tags and submit. Original reader synopses and credited artwork appear separately; reviews and other corrections are moderated.

Find a novel ↗

↻ Refresh a novel using Novel Updates

If a novel already has its Novel Updates link but its synopsis, tags, genres or cover reference are missing, open the novel, copy its existing NU series URL and choose Refresh details from Novel Updates. Paste the URL (or use the prefilled one) and tell us which fields need attention. The editor retrieves actual source information and reviews it before updating the existing entry; cover artwork requires separate rights approval.

Request a source refresh ↗

Only share descriptions and artwork you have the right to reuse. Reader contributions do not automatically replace the official catalogue description or cover.

Privacy & access

Two different spaces.

🌐 Public Conservatory

Open catalogue, search, collections, approved metadata, contributions and source links. No private EPUB downloads.

🔒 Private Conservatory

Access-approved members, stored EPUB editions and separate EPUB reading permissions. Private storage is never exposed publicly.
