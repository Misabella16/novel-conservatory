In Phase 6, this space will let you upload pictures of whole shelves, covers or spines, identify the individual books, and match them to existing records or add a separate physical copy.

Preview only: You can select and inspect local shelf pictures here, but nothing is uploaded, analyzed, matched, saved or shared yet. The private scanning service will be a separate, tested feature.
