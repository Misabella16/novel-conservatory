MEMBERS-ONLY SUBMISSIONS

Share an EPUB with the library

Found an edition missing from our shelves? Submit it privately for the owner's review. This is not a public upload, and submitting never gives other members permission to read the file.

Private & unlisted. Cloudflare login is required. Your file goes into a separate review queue, not the public catalogue or the shared reading shelf. Maximum 15 MB.

EPUB only, up to 15 MB. No PDF, ZIP archives of multiple books, or executable files.

The owner may review or reject the submission. If accepted, the owner will separately decide whether and how it is stored in the private library.
