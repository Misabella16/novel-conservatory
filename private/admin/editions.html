Bulk import

Owner-only. Choose EPUB files or a folder. EPUB titles are matched automatically to your entire catalogue. Missing novels receive private entries at upload time; only ambiguous titles need your selection. Each file remains a separate, selectable edition. Files over 30 MB upload in chunks. Recognizable EPUB covers are extracted and published automatically; later translation covers are saved as alternatives. New books are added to your private catalogue automatically and are not published on the public site.

For large batches, ambiguous matches are flagged. Ordinary uploads need no approval or confirmation. You can pause after a file. A retry after refreshing can resume previously uploaded chunks when the same EPUB is selected again.
