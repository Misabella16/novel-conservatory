Bulk import

Owner-only. Choose EPUB files or a folder. EPUB titles are matched automatically to your entire catalogue. Missing novels receive private entries at upload time; only ambiguous titles need your selection. Each file remains a separate, selectable edition. Files over 30 MB upload in chunks. Embedded EPUB synopsis and subject tags fill missing private-library metadata automatically. Recognizable covers are stored privately and never published to the public website. Existing hand-edited information is preserved. New books are added to your private catalogue automatically and are not published on the public site.

One click creates private novel entries and reusable book pages, attaches editions to existing canonical IDs, extracts embedded subject tags and covers, and checks a linked Novel Updates page only if trusted metadata was previously captured. Unsafe or ambiguous matches are reported, never forced.

For large batches, ambiguous matches are flagged. Ordinary uploads need no approval or confirmation. You can pause after a file. A retry after refreshing can resume previously uploaded chunks when the same EPUB is selected again.
