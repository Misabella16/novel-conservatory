Import from a Novel Updates page you can open

The automated server request was blocked (403), so this option retrieves existing metadata directly from a series page you open in your own browser. No AI writing, login credentials or third-party cookies are collected. It does not bypass any access challenge.

  1. On a computer, drag Capture Novel Updates to your browser bookmarks bar, or use and paste it into the address/URL of a new bookmark.
  2. Open a specific Novel Updates series page normally. Once its genres, tags and synopsis appear, click the Capture Novel Updates bookmark.
  3. Return here, paste the copied JSON metadata, then review and save it as unpublished.
